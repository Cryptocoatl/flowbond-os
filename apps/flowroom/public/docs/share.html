Your desk · Admin

Give someone their own way in.

Every person gets a personal link. You choose which documents it opens and whether they can write on them. Nobody sees a list of who else is here.

Three kinds of access

Pick by what you want back from the person. The difference is not what they can see — it is what they can do once they are looking.

Editor

Decide with you

For Kay, for counsel, for anyone whose “yes” actually counts.

  • Marks any paragraph: agreed, question, we already have this, different timing, not this
  • Leaves notes anyone in the room can read
Commenter

Weigh in, not sign off

For an advisor you want thinking out loud without their view reading as a decision.

  • Leaves notes
  • Cannot mark paragraphs agreed or rejected
Viewer

Just read it

For an investor, a board member, anyone being shown rather than consulted.

  • Reads the document, clean — no buttons, no marks, no notes, nothing to click
  • Never sees what anyone else wrote
Worth knowing

A Viewer's page has none of the marking controls in it at all — not hidden, not greyed out, not there. They cannot tell the room is annotatable, and they never see another person's questions. That is what makes it safe to send document 04 to someone outside the decision.

Make a link

Links you have made

Kept in this browser only, so you can find a link again without asking anyone. It is a list of what you sent — not a list of who has opened it.

How to send it

Send the link to one person. The link is the key: whoever holds it has the access you chose, so it should go in a direct message or an email to a single person, not into a group thread.

They do not need a password. They click, and they are in, greeted by name. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to install.

Their name is already on it. When an Editor marks a paragraph agreed, it says their name — because you put it on the key when you made it.

The one limit, stated plainly

Right now a link can't be switched off on its own. If one needs to be taken back — a person leaves, a message is forwarded — tell Steph, and every link is reissued at once. Everyone gets a new one; the old ones stop working.

Per-link revocation is the next thing to build here, and it is the thing that turns this from a document room into a full data room.
